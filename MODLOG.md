The old terrain handler sampled rendered Transform in Update, emitted damage for overlaps, and separated from only the closest tile using the final centre. Crossing a tile centre could therefore separate to its far side, including into an adjacent tile. Full crossings between checks were missed. This was a collision correctness issue, not a required cost of the spatial lookup optimization; Avian substeps did not cover the custom terrain handler.

Terrain contacts now capture authoritative Position at the start of each PhysicsSchedule step (after preparation/First), sweep the complete XY path against locally indexed expanded block bounds, and correct Position before writeback/interpolation. Simultaneous faces and sliding into a second surface are resolved together. Initial overlap recovery uses the union of candidate blocks. Bounce is applied only into a surface; damage/buffs use actual contacts with existing cooldowns on the shared virtual clock. Cobweb exclusion, snow/ice responses, explicit teleports and Flying/rocket exclusions are preserved. TNT and actor-versus-actor collision are outside this fix.
