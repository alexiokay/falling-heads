# Structure concepts

Three images were generated with OpenAI's built-in imagegen on 2026-10-24 for design review:

- `forest-apothecary.png`: hollow oak and forest apothecary.
- `underground-liftworks.png`: mining galleries, liftworks and crystal chamber.
- `desert-aqueduct-sanctuary.png`: aqueduct over a buried sanctuary; selected by the user.

These are concept illustrations, not screenshots or ready-made tilemaps. The first playable adaptation of the selected desert concept uses a 29-tile crossing, three arches, buried galleries and a reward alcove. Interactive water puzzles and the full illustrated architectural detail are not implemented.
